I NNOVEDA AI Legal-Reasoning Assistant for Ayurveda IP & Regulatory Guidance THE PROBLEM No Pre-Filing IP Check Ayurvedic patent filings often fail for hidden bars like Sec 3(p)/3(e)/3(d) — no tool flags this before money is spent on filing. Hallucinated Legal Answers Generic AI chatbots invent citations and give unguarded medical or legal opinions, risking bad filing decisions. Fragmented Statutory Knowledge Patents Act, Biodiversity Act and WIPO GRATK sit in separate silos, making cross-checking slow and error-prone. No Explainable Reasoning Existing tools give a verdict with no reasoning — innovators don't know why a formulation failed or what's missing. SOLUTION & UNIQUENESS Dual-Track AI Architecture Parallel classification (LLM) and RAG evidence retrieval run together, merged into one grounded verdict. Grounded Citation Engine Every claim is mapped to verified statutory text from a 258-chunk corpus; hallucinated citations are auto-dropped. Guardrail-First Design Medical-advice gate, scope validator and confidence threshold abstain rather than guess on shaky evidence. Evidence Gap Map Explains what's still unknown and links each missing fact back to the exact input field to fix. Botanical Entity Resolver A 150-species ontology resolves vernacular and Sanskrit plant names for accurate formulation matching.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY ‹#› @SIH Idea submission- Template InnoVeda FEASIBILITY Technical Feasibility Built entirely on proven, production-ready components — Next.js/React, FastAPI + Pydantic, Gemini 2.0 Flash and ChromaDB. No unproven infrastructure, so the prototype moves straight to a pilot. Data Feasibility The core knowledge base already exists: a 258-chunk statutory corpus (Patents Act, Biodiversity Act, WIPO GRATK) and a 150-species botanical ontology, so retrieval quality is not an open research risk. Operational Feasibility API-first architecture lets IP law firms, TKDL-linked researchers and Ayurveda startups plug the assistant into existing filing workflows without disrupting them. RISKS & MITIGATION Hallucinated or unverifiable citations The Citation Validator checks every claim against the statutory corpus and auto-drops anything unverified before it reaches the user. Ambiguous vernacular / Sanskrit plant names The 150-species Botanical Entity Resolver maps local names to Sanskrit, and flags any name it cannot confidently match. Statutes evolve across three separate regimes Patents Act, Biodiversity Act and WIPO GRATK are indexed as independent retrieval tracks, so one source updates without re-architecting the system. Scope creep into medical or legal advice The Medical-Advice Gate and Scope Validator hard-block out-of-scope queries before any LLM call, per the Guardrail-First design. Every safeguard above is already part of the working prototype (Track A/B pipeline, Guardrail Gate, Citation Validator) — feasibility here is engineering completion, not open research risk.
