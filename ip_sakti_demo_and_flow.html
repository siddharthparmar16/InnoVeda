STATUTORY VERDICT: NOT PATENTABLE AS CLAIMED

The formulation based on Haridra (Curcuma longa) and Maricha (Piper nigrum) is recognized as traditional knowledge. It is barred from product patenting under Indian Patent Law unless a novel synergistic extraction process is demonstrated.

Legal Reasoning & Statutory Breakdown

BARRED Patents Act 1970 - Section 3(p)
Traditional knowledge and aggregation of known medicinal properties of component herbs is explicitly excluded from patentability.
APPROVAL REQUIRED Biological Diversity Act 2002 - Section 6
Mandatory prior approval from National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) is strictly required before filing any IPR application using Indian biological resources.
DISCLOSURE MANDATE WIPO GRATK Treaty 2024 - Article 3
Requires mandatory disclosure of country of origin and associated traditional knowledge in international PCT filings across EU/US offices.
Recommended Viable Protection Routes
1. Novel Process / Method Patent
Focus the patent claims strictly on a unique, non-obvious bio-extraction method or synergistic temperature processing rather than the natural formulation.
2. Geographical Indication (GI)
If the raw materials (e.g. Lakadong Turmeric) are tied to a specific geographical region, pursue GI status for regional brand protection.
